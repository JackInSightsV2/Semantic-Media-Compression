# Patent Landscape Analysis

## Overview

This document analyzes the existing patent landscape relevant to semantic media compression technology. Understanding intellectual property considerations is crucial for developing a defensible technology position while avoiding infringement on existing patents.

## Key Patent Categories

### Media Compression Patents

#### Traditional Video Compression
**Relevant Patent Areas:**
- H.264/AVC compression standards (MPEG-LA patent pool)
- H.265/HEVC compression standards (multiple patent pools)
- VP9 and AV1 codec patents (Google, Alliance for Open Media)
- Proprietary compression algorithms (Dolby, DTS, others)

**Strategic Implications:**
- Semantic compression operates at different abstraction level
- May avoid traditional compression patent conflicts
- Could complement rather than replace existing standards

**Key Patent Holders:**

**Managed Patent Pools:**
