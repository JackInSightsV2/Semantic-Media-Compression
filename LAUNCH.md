# Commercial exploration — unvalidated research direction

Updated 8 October 2026. This page replaces the earlier investment-style pitch. Its claims of a $10B+ addressable market, fixed returns, sub-second performance and mathematical proof of plagiarism were not supported by project measurements or adequate primary evidence. They are withdrawn as factual claims. Earlier text remains in Git history.

## Potential product

A tool could help people inspect semantic overlap, compare adaptations and examine provenance. It would present evidence and uncertainty for human review. It would not decide copyright infringement, prove ownership from a hash, or automatically equate shared ideas with theft.

## What would validate an opportunity

- Document a narrow user problem and current workflow through interviews.
- Build a labelled comparison corpus containing authorised reuse, common ideas, paraphrases, independent creation and genuine copying allegations with expert review.
- Measure false positives, missed cases, reviewer time and cost against existing tools.
- Establish rights to process the material and jurisdiction-specific review procedures.
- Measure willingness to pay, adoption and support cost before calculating a market or return.

There is no verified market size, customer traction, revenue forecast or investment ask established by this repository. Model/API availability does not imply a defensible business or legal remedy.
