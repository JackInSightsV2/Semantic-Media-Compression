### What is Semantic Compression? (0:30-2:00)

"Let me explain semantic compression in the simplest way possible.

Traditional compression - like ZIP files or MP4s - they make files smaller by removing redundant data. But the content stays exactly the same.

Semantic compression is completely different. Instead of saving the *data*, we save the *meaning*.

Think about it like this: A 2-hour movie? That's usually 4-10 gigabytes of video data. 

With semantic compression, we extract:
- The story structure and plot progression
- Character personalities and how they develop
- The emotional beats and themes
- Visual composition and why each shot matters
- Cultural context and symbolism

All of that compresses down to about 6 megabytes of structured data - basically a JSON blueprint.

Then, an AI can read that blueprint and *recreate* the entire experience. Not a pixel-perfect copy, but something that preserves the meaning and intent."
