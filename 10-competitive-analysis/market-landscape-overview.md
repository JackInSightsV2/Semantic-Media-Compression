## Competitive Landscape Summary

The semantic media compression space intersects with several established technology domains:

- **AI Content Generation**: Companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, Stability AI
- **Media Compression**: Traditional players like Dolby, MPEG consortium members
- **Semantic Understanding**: Google, Microsoft, Meta AI research divisions
- **Creative Tools**: Adobe, Canva, emerging AI-native creative platforms
