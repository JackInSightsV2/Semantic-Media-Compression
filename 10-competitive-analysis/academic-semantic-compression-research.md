### Theoretical Foundations Research

#### Information Theory and Compression
**Research Areas:**
- **Rate-Distortion Theory**: Mathematical foundations of compression trade-offs
- **Semantic Information Theory**: Extensions of Shannon information theory to semantic content
- **Perceptual Information Theory**: Information theory adapted for human perception
- **Kolmogorov Complexity**: Algorithmic information theory applications to media

**Key Research Questions:**
1. How do researchers extend classical information theory to semantic content?
2. What mathematical frameworks exist for measuring semantic information content?
3. How is perceptual relevance incorporated into compression theory?
4. What are the theoretical limits of semantic compression?

- **Memory and Reconstruction**: How humans remember and reconstruct experiences
- **Cross-Cultural Perception**: Cultural differences in media interpretation

**Key Research Questions:**
