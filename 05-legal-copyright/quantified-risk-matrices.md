# Legal risk evidence matrix

Refreshed 8 October 2026. The previous 75% infringement probabilities, fixed mitigation deductions, settlement rates and 0% public-domain risk were unsupported. They are withdrawn as estimates; no dataset, denominator, jurisdiction or calibrated model established them.

| Question | Evidence to collect | Status without that evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| May the source be processed? | Licence/permission, rights holder, territory, intended use, relevant exceptions | Unresolved; do not infer permission from access |
| Does the source contain third-party material? | Work-level and component-level rights review | Unresolved; company ownership is not automatic |
| May outputs be distributed? | Licence scope, source dependence, human authorship and applicable rights | Separate assessment from input permission |
| Is similarity legally significant? | Protected expression, relevant context, access/copying evidence and applicable legal test | Similarity alone is insufficient |
| Is provenance trustworthy? | Identity evidence, signed statements, origin records and disputes | A hash alone only identifies matching bytes |
| What is the financial exposure? | Actual contracts, applicable remedies, insurance terms and professional assessment | No numeric prediction supplied by this repo |

If a future model estimates probabilities, publish its definition, population, data, legal scope, calibration and uncertainty. Never subtract arbitrary percentage points for mitigations or equate public-domain copyright status with absence of every legal risk.
