# Product Page Design Document ("Produce" / "Platform")

## Purpose

The Product page (likely `/produce` or `/product`) shifts focus from the *concept* of Semantic Compression to the *Platform* itself (`Semantical.Ink`). It explains **how** users interact with the tool to create, protect, and monetize their work.

**Goal**: Convert interested creators into users by showing the concrete workflow and the power of the platform.

---

## Page Structure

### Hero Section: "The Operating System for Your IP"
**Purpose**: Position Semantical.Ink not just as a tool, but as an infrastructure layer for creativity.

**Content**:
- **Headline**: "Manage, Protect, and Remix Your Creative DNA"
- **Subheadline**: "The first platform that treats your story as data. Upload your work, generate a Semantic Blueprint, and unlock a universe of derivatives."
- **Visual**: A high-fidelity dashboard mockup showing a "Project View" with a central asset (e.g., a book) connected to multiple outputs (script, comic, game) via "strands" of data.
- **CTA**: "Launch Studio" / "Start Free Trial"

**Image Requirements**:
- [ ] **Dashboard UI Mockup**: Dark mode, sleek, data-rich but clean. Shows a "Semantic Graph" of a story.

---

### Section 1: "The Semantic Blueprint" (The Engine)
**Purpose**: Explain the core artifact—the Semantic JSON—and why it's powerful.

**Content**:
- **Headline**: "Your Story, Structured."
- **Body**: "Our AI distills your content into a **Semantic JSON Blueprint**. This isn't just metadata; it's the source code of your narrative. It captures character arcs, emotional beats, and world-building rules in a format that machines can understand and creators can build upon."
- **Feature List**:
  - **Narrative Extraction**: Plot points, pacing, conflict.
  - **Character Profiles**: Personality, relationships, visual descriptors.
  - **Thematic Analysis**: Tone, mood, core messages.

**Visual**:
- [ ] **JSON Visualization**: A stylized code block or node graph where "raw code" transforms into "visual cards" (e.g., a JSON object `{ "character": "Hero" }` connecting to a character portrait).

---

### Section 2: "Protection by Default" (Story Protocol)
**Purpose**: Address the "AI Fear" (theft) with the "Web3 Solution" (provenance).

**Content**:
- **Headline**: "Proof of Authorship. Forever."
- **Body**: "Every Semantic Blueprint you create is automatically registered as an **IP Asset** on the blockchain via **Story Protocol**. You own the blueprint, and you own the rights to how it's used."
- **Key Benefits**:
  - **Immutable Record**: Time-stamped proof of creation.
  - **On-Chain Licensing**: Set rules for who can use your blueprint and how.
  - **Royalty Tracking**: (Future) Get paid when AI uses your work.

**Visual**:
- [ ] **Protection Shield / Chain**: A visual representation of a document being "locked" or "minted" into a block. Logos of **Story Protocol** should be visible but subtle.

---

### Section 3: "The Derivative Engine" (The Magic)
**Purpose**: Show the "Remix" capability.

**Content**:
- **Headline**: "One Source. Infinite Adaptations."
- **Body**: "Use your Semantic Blueprint to generate high-fidelity derivatives instantly. Because the AI understands the *meaning* of your story, adaptations stay true to the source material."
- **Workflow Steps**:
  1. **Select Blueprint**: Choose your "Space Opera Novel".
  2. **Choose Output**: Select "Graphic Novel Script" or "Anime Character Sheet".
  3. **Generate**: Watch as the semantic data reconstructs your story in a new format.

**Visual**:
- [ ] **Transformation Flow**: A linear or circular flow showing:
  `Novel (Input)` -> `Semantic Engine` -> `Screenplay (Output 1)` + `Comic (Output 2)` + `Audio Drama (Output 3)`

---

### Section 4: "IP Management Dashboard" (The Tool)
**Purpose**: Show the actual UI users will spend time in.

**Content**:
- **Headline**: "Complete Control Over Your Creative Assets"
- **Body**: "Manage your entire IP portfolio in one place. Track versions, view derivative chains, and manage access rights."
- **UI Highlights to Call Out**:
  - **Asset Library**: Grid view of all uploaded works.
  - **Genealogy Tree**: Visual tree showing which derivatives came from which parent.
  - **Marketplace Integration**: One-click publishing to the Semantical Marketplace.

**Visual**:
- [ ] **"Genealogy" UI**: A screenshot showing a "Family Tree" of content. Parent node (Original) branching into child nodes (Translations, Adaptations).

## Visual Style Guide for Product Page
- **Tech-Forward**: Use darker backgrounds (Brand Dark) with neon accents (Brand Cyan/Pink) to feel like a "Pro Tool".
- **Data-Viz Heavy**: Use lines, nodes, and connecting graphs to emphasize "connection" and "structure".
- **Clean UI**: Screenshots should look simplified but functional.
