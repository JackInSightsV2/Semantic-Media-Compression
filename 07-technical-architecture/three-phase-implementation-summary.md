Updated 8 October 2026. The earlier 2024–2027 / 2027–2030 / 2030+ dates were scenarios, not delivery forecasts. These pathways can overlap; one does not mathematically require the others.

| Pathway | Current status | Gate for advancing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human-readable fact blueprints | Implemented for a small text pilot | Independent held-out retention, unsupported-fact checks and realistic cost baselines |
| Vector-assisted representations | Research/design proposal in this repo; embeddings exist elsewhere today | Demonstrate added value over text at matched complete storage and quality budgets |
| Learned/native semantic codecs | External research field and longer-term project hypothesis | Working encoder/decoder, shared-model accounting and reproducible rate/quality comparisons |

Storing vectors in JSON does not make the meanings exact or the adaptation culturally correct. `original + context_vector` is an illustrative operation, not a guarantee. JSON syntax alone does not specify units, vocabulary, decoder behaviour or fidelity.

The old claims of 1000:1/10,000:1 production compression, perfectly adapted knowledge bases and pixel-perfect cultural transformation were unsupported targets. They are not milestones already passed.
