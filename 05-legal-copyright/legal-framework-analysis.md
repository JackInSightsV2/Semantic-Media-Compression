## Core distinctions

US copyright protects original expression, not ideas, procedures or concepts. A high semantic similarity score is not a statutory infringement test. Conversely, paraphrasing or transforming through a semantic representation does not automatically authorise reuse of protected expression. [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/).

Rights to source material, rights to process it, and rights in generated output are separate questions. The US Copyright Office's 2025 report distinguishes human-authored expression and arrangements from material generated without sufficient human control; prompts alone do not establish authorship. This is not a worldwide rule or automatic source clearance. [Official report announcement](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html).

The UK government's March 2026 copyright/AI report is policy and evidence work, not itself a licence to process protected works. Apply the relevant law and permissions to the actual material, territory and use. [UK report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/report-and-impact-assessment-on-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence/report-on-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence).

## Evidence records

A useful registry could store signed identity assertions, source/asset hashes, licence documents and references, timestamps, changes and dispute status. A blockchain records submissions under its consensus assumptions; it does not make false assertions true or eliminate challenges to title. Token ownership is distinct from copyright ownership. [Joint USPTO/USCO NFT study](https://www.copyright.gov/policy/nft-study/).

## Project limits

- No 6.2% “precedent coverage” estimate or confidence interval has a reproducible sampling method here; those claims are withdrawn.
- No 85%, 90% or 95% similarity threshold determines infringement or shifts a legal burden.
- The risk matrices are unvalidated planning aids, not actuarial/legal forecasts.
- A work's public-domain status requires checking the actual work, edition, recording, territory and use. A blanket date rule does not clear every form of media.
- Templates elsewhere in this repository are historical drafts requiring review, not ready-to-deploy compliance instruments.

Before using a corpus, record provenance and the basis for each permitted operation. Treat privacy, contractual restrictions, attribution and other rights separately from copyright. Refer to the specific jurisdiction and facts rather than guaranteeing compliance from an architecture.
