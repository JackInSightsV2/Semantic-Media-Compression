## The Economic Change

Semantic media compression allows basic changes in how value is created, distributed, and captured in media-related industries:

**Massive Cost Savings**: 1000:1+ compression cuts storage and bandwidth costs.
**Quality for Everyone**: Anyone can create and share high-quality content.
**New Ways to Make Money**: Collaborative creation and AI experiences.

### The Economic Hypothesis: When Does This Make Business Sense?

**Critical Insight**: Semantic compression isn't about replacing traditional distribution - it's about enabling **adaptation at scale**.

**Hypothetical Break-even analysis shows** semantic compression becomes economically compelling at **3-5 variants**:

| Variants | Traditional Cost | Semantic Cost | Winner |
|----------|-----------------|---------------|--------|
| 1 version | Low | High | Traditional |
| 3-5 versions | High | Moderate | Approaching parity |
| 10+ versions | Very High | Moderate | Semantic (10× better) |
| 50+ versions | Prohibitive | Viable | Semantic (100× better) |

**This defines the theoretical market**: Educational content, corporate training, documentary localization - anywhere adaptation creates value.
