# Quantified Risk Assessment Matrices

## Overview

This document provides detailed, quantified risk assessment matrices with specific probability calculations, financial impact projections, and mitigation cost analyses for different semantic media compression scenarios. These matrices enable data-driven decision-making about legal risk management strategies.

## Comprehensive Risk Probability Matrix

### High-Impact Litigation Scenarios

**Major Entertainment Studio Enforcement Action**
- Base Probability: 75% for unlicensed commercial use
- Probability Modifiers:
  - Licensed content: -60% (15% final probability)
  - Fair use documentation: -25% (50% final probability)
  - Educational use: -40% (35% final probability)
  - Public domain content: -75% (0% final probability)
- Expected Timeline: 18-36 months from initial complaint

- Trial Verdict Range: $5M-$100M+ plus injunctive relief

- Base Probability: 85% for unlicensed music compression
- Probability Modifiers:
  - ASCAP/BMI licensing: -70% (15% final probability)
  - Mechanical license compliance: -50% (35% final probability)

- Expected Timeline: 12-24 months from initial complaint
- Settlement Probability: 80% before formal litigation
- Average Settlement Range: $500K-$5M

**International Copyright Enforcement**
- Base Probability: 45% varies significantly by jurisdiction

- Lower-Risk Jurisdictions: Many developing nations (15-25%)
- Cross-Border Complexity Multiplier: +25% probability
- Expected Timeline: 6-18 months per jurisdiction

- Probability for Non-Compliant Platforms: 90%

- Base Probability: 60% for platforms enabling user uploads

- Defense Costs: $500K-$5M regardless of outcome
