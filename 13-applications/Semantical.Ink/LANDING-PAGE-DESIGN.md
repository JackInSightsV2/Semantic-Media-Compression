# Landing Page Design Document

## Purpose

The landing page serves as the **vision alignment tool** for the team and the **onboarding experience** for creators. It explains semantic compression in simple, visual terms and guides users into the product.

**Goal**: Make complex semantic compression concepts accessible to indie creators who may not have technical backgrounds.

---

## Page Structure

### Hero Section
**Purpose**: Immediate value proposition and clear call-to-action

**Content**:
- **Headline**: "Turn Your Creative Work Into Its Essence"
- **Subheadline**: "Semantic compression captures the meaning, story, and soul of your content—not just the pixels or words"
- **Visual**: Large hero image showing transformation (e.g., comic book → semantic blueprint → new formats)
- **CTA Button**: "See How It Works" (scrolls to next section) + "Get Started" (goes to Produce page)

**Image Requirements**:
- Hero illustration showing: Original Content → Semantic Blueprint → Regenerated Content
- Should feel creative and inspiring, not technical
- Use warm, inviting colors

---

### Section 1: "What is Semantic Compression?" (Simple Explanation)

**Purpose**: Answer the fundamental question in plain language

**Content Structure**:

#### The Problem (Visual Comparison)
**Left Side - Traditional Compression**:
- Image: File size reduction (100MB → 10MB)
- Text: "Traditional compression shrinks files but loses quality"
- Visual: Pixelated/blurry image or compressed audio waveform

**Right Side - Semantic Compression**:
- Image: Content essence preserved
- Text: "Semantic compression captures what your content *means*"
- Visual: Clear, meaningful representation

**Image Requirements**:
- Side-by-side comparison illustration
- Show traditional vs semantic approach
- Use familiar content types (comic, novel, art)

#### The Core Concept
**Headline**: "Your Story, Not Your File"

**Content**:
- "Imagine you wrote a 300-page novel. Traditional compression would shrink the file size, but you'd still have 300 pages of text."
- "Semantic compression asks: What's the *story*? Who are the *characters*? What are the *themes*? It captures the essence—the blueprint—that can recreate your novel, turn it into a comic, adapt it for different cultures, or transform it into new formats."

**Visual Elements**:
- **Large Diagram**: Novel → Semantic Blueprint (JSON-like structure) → Multiple Outputs
- **Icons/Illustrations**: 
  - Book icon → Blueprint icon → Multiple format icons (comic, animation, etc.)
- **Process Flow**: Visual timeline showing the transformation

**Image Requirements**:
- Flow diagram: Content → Blueprint → Regeneration
- Use icons for different content types
- Show multiple output possibilities
- Keep it simple and colorful

---

### Section 2: "How It Works" (3-Step Visual Process)

**Purpose**: Break down the process into digestible steps

#### Step 1: Upload Your Content
**Visual**: Large image showing upload interface or content being uploaded

**Content**:
- "Upload your novel, comic, artwork, or any creative content"
- "We support: PDFs, images, text files, and more"

**Image Requirements**:
- Screenshot or illustration of upload interface
- Show various file types (book, comic, art)
- Friendly, approachable design

#### Step 2: AI Extracts the Essence
**Visual**: Animated or static diagram showing AI analyzing content

**Content**:
- "Our AI reads your content like a human would"
- "It identifies:"
  - **Narrative**: The story structure and plot
  - **Characters**: Who they are, their relationships, their arcs
  - **Themes**: Core messages and emotional tone
  - **Visual Style** (for comics/art): Color palettes, composition, artistic techniques

**Image Requirements**:
- **Diagram showing AI "reading" content**:
  - Content on left
  - AI brain/processing icon in middle
  - Semantic dimensions radiating out (Narrative, Character, Theme, Visual)
- **Visual breakdown**: Show how a comic page gets analyzed
  - Original comic panel
  - Overlay showing: characters identified, narrative beats, visual style
- Use icons for each dimension

#### Step 3: Create Your Semantic Blueprint
**Visual**: Interactive or static view of semantic blueprint

**Content**:
- "Your content becomes a structured blueprint—a recipe that preserves everything that matters"
- "This blueprint can:"
  - Be stored forever (tiny file size)
  - Be shared and licensed
  - Generate new content in different formats
  - Adapt to different cultures and styles

**Image Requirements**:
- **Blueprint visualization**: 
  - Show JSON-like structure (but make it visual, not code-heavy)
  - Use cards or sections for: Narrative, Characters, Themes, Visual
  - Make it look like a creative tool, not a technical document
- **Before/After comparison**:
  - Original: Large file, one format
  - Blueprint: Small file, infinite possibilities

## Visual Design Principles

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Creative, inspiring colors (not too technical)
- **Accent**: Use color to differentiate sections
- **Neutral**: Clean backgrounds for readability

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Bold, friendly, approachable
- **Body**: Readable, not too small
- **Avoid**: Technical jargon, code-like fonts

### Imagery Style
- **Illustrations**: Custom illustrations preferred over stock photos
- **Diagrams**: Simple, clear, not overly technical
- **Icons**: Consistent icon set throughout
- **Screenshots**: Real product screenshots (when available)

### Layout
- **Spacing**: Generous white space
- **Sections**: Clear visual separation
- **Scrolling**: Smooth scroll experience
- **Mobile**: Responsive, touch-friendly

## Content Guidelines

### Tone
- **Friendly**: Approachable, not intimidating
- **Inspiring**: Exciting about possibilities
- **Simple**: Plain language, no jargon
- **Creator-focused**: Speak to creators' needs

### Language Examples

**Good**:
- "Your story becomes a blueprint"
- "Capture what your content means"
- "Create new formats from the same essence"

**Avoid**:
- "Vector embeddings"
- "Semantic extraction algorithms"
- "Multi-dimensional similarity matrices"
- Technical implementation details

### Metaphors to Use
- **Blueprint**: Like an architect's blueprint for a building
- **Recipe**: Like a recipe that can make different dishes
- **DNA**: Like DNA that contains all the information
- **Essence**: Like distilling the essence of your content

## User Flow

### Path 1: Quick Explorer
1. Lands on page
2. Scrolls through sections
3. Clicks "See How It Works" (scrolls to Section 2)
4. Reads through process
5. Clicks "Get Started" → Goes to Produce page

### Path 2: Deep Diver
1. Lands on page
2. Reads each section carefully
3. Views all images and examples
4. Understands full value proposition
5. Clicks "Get Started" → Goes to Produce page

### Path 3: Skeptic
1. Lands on page
2. Reads "What is Semantic Compression?"
3. Needs more explanation
4. Scrolls to "How It Works"
5. Sees use cases
6. Convinced → Clicks "Get Started"
