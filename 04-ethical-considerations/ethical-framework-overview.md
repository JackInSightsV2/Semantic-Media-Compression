## Core Ethical Principles

### Authenticity and Truth
- Clearly label regenerated content as AI-generated
- Preserve original meaning and intent during regeneration
- Prevent misuse for creating misleading or false content
- Maintain attribution to original creators

### Consent and Control
- Get explicit permission before compressing someone's content
- Allow people to opt out of semantic compression systems
- Give users control over how their content is used and regenerated
- Respect intellectual property and creator rights

### Cultural Respect
- Avoid stereotypes and cultural misrepresentation
- Ensure cultural adaptations are respectful and accurate
- Include cultural communities in validation processes
- Prevent cultural appropriation through AI generation

### Privacy Protection
- Protect personal information in semantic compression
- Secure storage and transmission of compressed content
- Allow users to delete their compressed data
- Respect privacy laws across different jurisdictions
